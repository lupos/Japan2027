Planning & Logistics Reference

Recommended 2-Step Strategy:
  1. Nov / Dec 2026 (5–6 Months Out): Book your 3 Mt. Fuji-side (富士山側) rooms at #4 Konansou on a Standard Flexible Plan. Konansou allows 100% FREE cancellation up until 8 days before check-in.
  2. Jan / Feb 1, 2027 (3–4 Months Out): Try to book your 3 rooms at #1 Shuhokaku Kogetsu the morning May 2027 dates open. If you get #1, immediately cancel #4 online for $0 penalty (3+ months ahead of the cutoff). If #1 sells out, you already have #4 locked in!
Official Standard Cancellation Cutoffs (Standard Plans):
• #4 Konansou: Free until 8 days prior (7–4 days: 20% • 3–2 days: 30% • 1 day: 50% • Same-day: 100%)
• #1 Shuhokaku Kogetsu: Free until 22 days prior (21–15 days: 10% • 14–8 days: 20% • 7–2 days: 30%)
• #2 Shotokan: Free until 8–15 days prior (some open-air suite plans start at 14 days: 30%)
• #3 Kaneyamaen / Bessho Sasa: Free until 11 days prior (10–2 days: 30% • 1 day: 50%)
• #5 Fugaku Hanabusa: Free until 11 days prior (10–4 days: 10% • 3–2 days: 30%)
• #6 Fugaku Gunjo & #7 Zen: Free until 8–15 days prior on standard plans (avoid さき楽45 / 早割 early-bird discounted plans).
🗻 Route A — Lake Kawaguchiko (#1 Kogetsu, #3 Bessho Sasa, #4 Konansou)

Tokyo (Shinjuku/Tokyo Stn) → Highway Bus or JR Fuji Excursion Express (1 hr 45 min) → Kawaguchiko Stn (free ryokan shuttle) → Fujikyu Express Bus (1 hr 30 min, reserve seats 1 mo ahead) → Mishima Shinkansen Stn → Tokaido Shinkansen (2 hr 00 min) → Kyoto / Osaka.

🚅 Route B — Suruga Bay / Izu (#2 Shotokan, #5 Fugaku Hanabusa, #6 Fugaku Gunjo)

Tokyo Stn → Tokaido Shinkansen (45 min) → Mishima Stn → 25–30 min taxi or local train → Ryokan → Back to Mishima Stn → Tokaido Shinkansen (2 hr 00 min) → Kyoto / Osaka.

💡 Pro-Tip (Return Leg & Luggage): Doing the ryokan stopover on the return leg (Kyoto → Ryokan → Tokyo) lets you recover after a week of walking 20,000+ steps/day—and you can ship your big suitcases directly from Kyoto to your final Tokyo hotel via Takkyubin, bringing only a light overnight bag to the ryokan.
✕ Kozantei Ubuya (湖山亭うぶや — Kawaguchiko):

All 20 private in-room open-air baths use heated tap/spring water (沸かし湯)—NOT natural onsen water (YouTube Proof ↗).

✕ Fufu Kawaguchiko & THE KUKUNA:

100% of their open-air onsen rooms are Western Twin Beds only (no tatami/futon rooms), and lower-floor Fufu rooms have trees blocking the base of Mt. Fuji.

✕ Wakakusa no Yado Maruei & La Vista Kawaguchiko:

In-room baths are heated tap water (沸かし湯); Maruei's open-air bath rooms face the lake looking away from Mt. Fuji.

✕ Hotel Green Plaza Hakone & Ashinoko Hanaori:

In-room baths are heated tap water (沸かし湯), dining is a noisy buffet hall, and Hanaori has no Mt. Fuji view from the rooms.
