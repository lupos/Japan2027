4. Transit & Routing (Tokyo ↔ Ryokan ↔ Kyoto)

🗻 Route A — Lake Kawaguchiko / Fujiyoshida (#1 Kogetsu, #3 Bessho Sasa, #4 Konansou)

Tokyo (Shinjuku/Tokyo Stn) → Highway Bus or JR Fuji Excursion Express (1 hr 45 min) → Kawaguchiko / Fujisan Stn (free ryokan shuttle) → Fujikyu Express Bus (1 hr 30 min) → Mishima Shinkansen Stn → Tokaido Shinkansen (2 hr 00 min) → Kyoto / Osaka.

🚅 Route B — Suruga Bay / Izu (#2 Shotokan, #5 Fugaku Hanabusa, #6 Fugaku Gunjo)

Tokyo Stn → Tokaido Shinkansen (45 min) → Mishima Stn → 25–30 min taxi or Izuhakone local train → Ryokan → Back to Mishima Stn → Tokaido Shinkansen (2 hr 00 min) → Kyoto / Osaka.

💡 Pro-Tip (Outbound vs. Return Leg): Putting the ryokan on the return trip (Kyoto → Ryokan → Tokyo) is a huge favorite for 6-person groups: after a week of walking 20,000+ steps a day in Kyoto/Osaka, soaking in a private Mt. Fuji hot spring with a Kaiseki feast is the ultimate recovery—and you can ship your large suitcases directly from your Kyoto hotel to your final Tokyo hotel via Takkyubin, bringing only a light overnight bag to the ryokan.

⚠️ Research Methodology & Disqualifications

6. Appendix: Popular "Listicle Traps" We Eliminated

Cross-referencing official Japanese room specifications (客室詳細) and hot spring disclosures (温泉表記) immediately disqualified several of the internet's most heavily promoted English-blog properties:

✕ Kozantei Ubuya (湖山亭うぶや — Lake Kawaguchiko):

Featured on almost every English travel blog. Official Japanese specs confirm all 20 private in-room open-air baths use heated tap/spring water (沸かし湯)—NOT natural volcanic onsen water (only the communal bath uses onsen water). (Watch YouTube Proof).

✕ Hotel Green Plaza Hakone & Hakone Ashinoko Hanaori (はなをり):

In-room open-air baths are heated tap water (沸かし湯), dining is a noisy buffet hall, and Hanaori does not see Mt. Fuji from the rooms.

✕ Wakakusa no Yado Maruei (若草の宿 丸栄) & La Vista Kawaguchiko:

In-room open-air baths are heated tap water (沸かし湯); Maruei's open-air bath rooms face the lake, looking away from Mt. Fuji.

✕ Fufu Kawaguchiko (ふふ河口湖) & THE KUKUNA:

Both have real natural onsen in the rooms, but 100% of their open-air onsen rooms are Western Twin Beds only (no tatami/futon option), and lower-floor rooms at Fufu have forest trees blocking the base of Mt. Fuji.
